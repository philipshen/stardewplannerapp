Stardew Planner support

A free, unofficial mobile planner made by phil.

Start a farm

Tap New farm on the home screen, choose a layout, and create your plan. The tutorial introduces the touch controls. You can replay it from the editor’s gear menu.

Keep a backup

Your plans save on your device. Open the gear menu and export a plan file to keep a separate backup or move a plan to another device. On the destination device, tap Import on the home screen and select that file. An exported image is a picture of your farm, not an editable backup.

Radio and sound

Open Radio from the home screen or the editor’s gear menu. Choose an activity station, seasonal radio, or your own music. Check the radio volume and your device’s volume if you cannot hear it. On iPhone, the app respects silent mode. Playback pauses while the app is in the background.

Get help

Contact @thicc__cheney on Twitter. Include your device model, app version, and the steps that led to the problem. Share only files you are comfortable sending.
